# Overheard — allotment gate, Sunday

Two people arguing, mildly, about the strip of weeds outside the gate. One of them
ended it with:

> "It's not that it's untidy. It's that it's untidy *on purpose*, and that's what
> people can't stand."

Which is the whole thing, really. The objection to pavement plants is almost never
botanical. It is about legibility — a verge that looks unmanaged reads as a place
nobody is responsible for.

Where this goes: the winter essay, if there is one. It is the missing middle of the
argument I keep failing to finish — the one about tidiness as a moral category.

Do not attribute. Two strangers at a gate, no names, and I only heard the last line.
