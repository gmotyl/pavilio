# Post idea — the footbridge buddleia

Walked the cut on the way in. The *Buddleja davidii* on the disused footbridge is
now taller than the handrail — call it 140 cm, up from maybe 90 cm in April.

It is growing out of the expansion joint at the north abutment. Not soil. A seam of
grit and pigeon debris about 3 cm wide.

The angle: everyone writes buddleia as an invader. The interesting story is that it
is a **cliff plant** doing exactly what it evolved to do, and a bridge joint is a
cliff. Nothing about it is out of place. We are the ones who built the cliff.

Needs: photo of the whole bridge for scale, plus a close-up of the joint. Go back on
a dry morning — the north side is in shade until about ten.

Working title: `something like a cliff`
