# Context — Pavement Botany

> Example content. *Pavement Botany* is a fictional blog invented for this starter kit.

Fieldnotes on the plants that grow where nobody planted them: kerbs, cracks, gutters, tarmac seams. All fieldwork is set in the invented town of **Ashgate** and its river district, **Lower Ashgate**.

## Publishing vocabulary

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **fieldnote** | Short post from a single walk. Date, weather, what was growing. 300–600 words. |
| **essay** | Long post, argued rather than observed. 1500+ words. Rare — four or five a year. |
| **survey** | A repeated count along a fixed route. Data, not prose. Published as a table. |
| **roundup** | Seasonal summary post. Four a year, one per season. |
| **stub** | A post that exists as a title and two sentences. Lives in `drafts/`, may never ship. |
| **the Verge Report** | The monthly email. Subject to [ADR 0003](adr/0003-retire-the-verge-report.md). |

## Recurring subjects

- **the footbridge** — the disused rail footbridge over the Ashgate cut. Best buddleia in town.
- **the Kerb Walk** — fixed 1.2 km survey route, walked the first Saturday of every month since mid-2025.
- **the allotment gate** — where the good overheard lines happen.
- **crack class** — informal size grading for a pavement fissure: hairline, seam, gap, breach.

## House style

- Titles are lowercase. No colons in titles.
- Latin binomial on first mention, italic, then the common name for the rest of the post.
- Never publish an ID without a photo. If there's no photo it's "something like" and stays hedged.
- Measurements metric. Distances to one decimal place, plant heights to the nearest 5 cm.
- No second person. The reader is not being instructed.
- Dates in prose are written out ("14 March"), never numeric.
- Species counts always say the route and the date, or they mean nothing.

## What this project is not

Not an identification guide, not a campaign, not gardening. If a draft starts telling
people what to do with their front garden, it has drifted and gets moved back to `drafts/`.
