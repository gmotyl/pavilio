# 0003 — Retire the Verge Report

## Status

**Proposed** — 6 July 2026. Not decided. Revisit at the autumn roundup.

## Context

The Verge Report is the monthly email: a short intro, links to whatever shipped,
one photo. It has run for eleven months.

Observations from the year:

- Writing it costs roughly an evening. That is one fieldnote's worth of time.
- The intro paragraph is the only original writing in it, and it is the part
  readers reply to. The link list is a worse version of the archive page.
- Subscriber growth has been flat since the winter. Opens are steady, replies are
  rare but good.
- Twice the email went out with the wrong month in the subject line, which suggests
  I am not paying attention while making it.

The honest summary: it is not failing, it is just the least interesting thing this
project produces, and it consumes the same evenings as the most interesting thing.

## Decision (proposed)

Stop the monthly email. Replace it with one **seasonal** letter, sent with each
roundup — four a year instead of twelve, and no separate writing task, because the
roundup already exists per [notes/seasonal-cadence.md](../notes/seasonal-cadence.md).

The intro paragraph — the part that works — becomes the roundup's opening.

## Consequences

- Eight evenings a year returned to fieldnotes.
- The reply channel narrows from monthly to seasonal. This is the real cost and the
  reason this is still Proposed rather than Accepted.
- Anyone subscribed for monthly cadence gets something different from what they
  signed up for. Needs an explicit note in the final monthly issue.
- "the Verge Report" as a name may not survive the change — a seasonal letter
  attached to a roundup might just be the roundup.
- If it goes ahead, [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md) needs its vocabulary table updated.
