# Seasonal cadence

Pavement Botany is not a weekly blog and stopped pretending to be one in the first
autumn. The plants have a calendar; the writing follows it. This note is the shape of
the year, written down so the quiet months stop feeling like failure.

## The year

| Season | What is out there | What gets written |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | germination, the first crack colonists | fieldnotes, high volume — the good months |
| Summer | buddleia, everything at full height | fieldnotes plus the year's one long essay, usually |
| Autumn | seed, senescence, the tidy-up crews | roundups, surveys, archive work |
| Winter | almost nothing green, structure visible | drafts, rewriting, the stub review |

Four roundups a year, one per season, published in the first week of the following
one. They are the only fixed deadline in the project.

## The Kerb Walk

Same 1.2 km, first Saturday of every month, twelve fixed count points. Started
mid-2025, so the second full year of comparable data completes this autumn.

Rules that keep the data honest:

- Walk it even in February when the count is three species. The zeroes are the point.
- Count at the point, not near it. If a point has been resurfaced, it stays in the
  series as a zero with a note — do not quietly move it to a better patch of kerb.
- Survey posts publish the table and shut up. Interpretation belongs in the roundup.

## Stub review

Every season, before the roundup, read everything in `drafts/`
(see [ADR 0001](../adr/0001-drafts-live-in-the-repo.md)) and sort each item:

- **promote** — has an angle and a photo, move to `ready/`
- **keep** — still interesting, still not ready, leave it
- **kill** — delete it, and stop feeling responsible for it

The kill option is the whole reason the review exists. Roughly a third go each time.
The count has stabilised around ten to twelve stubs, which seems to be the natural
carrying capacity of one person's attention.

## Why the quiet months are load-bearing

Winter produces almost no fieldnotes and most of the year's actual writing. The
essays get drafted when there is nothing to go and look at. Trying to write an essay
in May has never once worked — there is always a better reason to be outside.
