# Two mics instead of one?

**2026-07-12 23:34**

Was reading about how you get direction out of a stereo pair — time difference
of arrival between two capsules a known distance apart. Perch box has exactly
one mic and therefore no idea where anything is.

If Box 1 had a pair 20 cm apart I could get a rough bearing per detection. Which
would mean I could tell "at the feeder" from "in next door's tree", and half my
tally inflation problem is exactly that confusion.

Reasons this is probably a trap:

- Bearing only, no distance. A bearing without a range is a line, not a spot.
- Needs the two channels genuinely sample-synced. My current capture path does
  not promise that at all.
- Every clip doubles in size, straight into the retention budget from `0002`.
- The enclosure is drilled for one mic and sealed with a lot of silicone.

And the honest version: this doesn't answer any question I've actually asked.
Compare with `adr/0003` — a second box at the back fence tells me about a
different habitat, which is a real question. Direction finding tells me a robin
was slightly to the left.

Parking it. Revisit if the Box 2 experiment says the garden really is two
different places.
